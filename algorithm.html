Basic quantum gates

GateInputOutputPurpose
I (Identity)|0>|0>Leaves the qubit unchanged.
X (NOT)|0>|1>Flips zero to one.
X (NOT)|1>|0>Flips one to zero.
H (Hadamard)|0>(|0> + |1>) / sqrt(2)Creates an equal superposition.
H (Hadamard)|1>(|0> - |1>) / sqrt(2)Creates superposition with a phase difference.
